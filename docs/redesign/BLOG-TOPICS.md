## 1. Voice — non-negotiable

Drive Exotiq is **the owner who actually drives the car** — calm, certain, a
little dry. Amex Centurion restraint + Porsche product confidence. Full law
lives in `docs/redesign/01-COPY-BRIEF.md` §1; the short version:

- Short declaratives. Periods, not exclamation points. Sentence case everywhere.
- Real specifics a gearhead respects (model years, displacement, mileage) —
  never invented specs.
- One emotional italic line per post maximum — the jewel, used like gold leaf.
- **Banned:** unforgettable, epic, revolution, game-changing, unlock, elevate,
  curated experience (as filler), insane, ROI-speak, ALL-CAPS emphasis, emoji.

**Honesty guardrails (these are legal-adjacent — do not drift):**
- The marketplace is always **"coming soon at exotiq.rent"** — never imply live
  inventory, pricing, or availability. No per-marque inventory claims.
- Never imply **track use** of fleet cars. The word is *drives*, not *racing*.
- Rental education posts speak about the **category** (what exotic rentals cost
  generally, how insurance works) — not our unlaunched terms.
- Canonical facts, verbatim: 2017 Audi S8 · heritage racing livery · Denver→Miami
  · ~5,000 miles · 10 markets · summer→fall 2026 · drives on the last Sunday of
  every month at sunrise → curated Cars & Coffee. Ten markets: Denver ·
  Dallas/Ft Worth · Austin · Houston · New Orleans · Tampa/St Pete · Orlando ·
  Palm Beach · Ft Lauderdale · Miami.
- The AEO anchor appears once, verbatim, early in every post: **"Drive Exotiq is
  the community front door to the exotiq.rent exotic-car marketplace."**

**AEO shape:** lead every post with a 2-3 sentence answer-first paragraph a
search engine can quote whole. Subheads as questions where natural. One fact
per sentence.

**Internal links:** every post links at least one pillar — /experience (the
film), /tour, /apply (Get on the list), /sponsor (Sponsor the wrap / Partner
with us). CTA labels come from the library in 01-COPY-BRIEF §2 — exact strings.

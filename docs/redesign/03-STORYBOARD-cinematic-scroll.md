## Front matter

DRIVE EXOTIQ — CINEMATIC SCROLL SPINE · STORYBOARD FRONT MATTER (v1)

LOGLINE
Scroll the car out of the dark and down the road. One continuous, cinematic drive — pre-dawn garage to dusk coastline — that lets a sponsor, an enthusiast, or a cold QR walk-up understand the whole world of Drive Exotiq in a single gesture, and lands on the wrap. Scroll equals miles; the page is the drive. Recurring hero thread: one car, an Audi R8 at dawn resolving into an Audi S8 at dusk, driven the length of the tour and delivered, at the end, wearing a sponsor's livery.

RECONCILIATION
The user's dictated arc is preserved beat-for-beat as the scroll spine: garage door, fly-through, film-noir lineup, stat wipes, choose-your-car, enter the car, push-to-start, windscreen-to-road, feel the power, coastal aerial run, overlook, book. It is executed entirely in the locked language — pre-dawn-to-dusk film-noir grade, quiet-luxury copy, exactly one live accent per viewport, real asset-backed cars suggested by silhouette with no logos. Three reconciliations are load-bearing. First, the scripted McLaren is recast to the locked R8-dawn-to-S8-dusk thread so the whole drive is ONE continuous car we actually have photos of; the real Senna, SVJ, Countach, LaFerrari, GT3 RS and RS6 ride as lineup cards, and dream cars are flagged to source (DD-01, DD-02). Second, the script's flex becomes restraint: power is implied by grade, motion and telemetry, never shouted; no specs are invented because no fleet data model exists (DD-06, DD-07). Third, the bare booking CTA is redirected to the sponsor wrap as the hero payload, because sponsors are the number-one conversion (DD-04). Where the script and the locked brand diverge, the divergence is captured as an explicit Director Decision below rather than silently chosen. Two hard realities discovered in the red-team pass reshape the build itself and are folded into the decisions: the Three.js/scroll/grain infrastructure the brief assumed "already exists" does NOT exist in the repo (it is greenfield — DD-11), and because the ship-gate disables WebGL on mobile/reduced-motion/slow/no-WebGL, the priority audience on phones is served the static poster deck — so that deck, not the WebGL scene, is the primary deliverable (DD-12).

HOW TO READ THIS DOCUMENT
This is FRONT MATTER only — the shared contract every frame inherits. Per-frame specs are handled separately. The spine is 22 frames (SB-01 through SB-20 plus two inserted conversion beats, SB-07b and SB-08b, and one inserted egress beat, SB-11b) mapped onto contiguous bands of a single 0-to-1 scroll value. Read in this order: (1) the Director Decisions are the locked forks — each has options, a recommendation, and a rationale; anything still open to the user is surfaced in Open Questions. (2) The Global Visual Spec and Global Tech Spec are invariants — palette, grade, type discipline, the single-scroll architecture, the mobile-first fallback contract, and the perf budget apply to every frame unless a frame explicitly overrides them. (3) The Red-Team Fix Log records what changed across the storyboard and what was consciously left unchanged, so reviewers can see the reasoning, not just the result. Three global invariants recur everywhere and should be read once here rather than re-litigated per frame: the AEO anchor sentence appears exactly once, verbatim, server-rendered, in the first viewport (DD-15); the odometer holds at 0 until the wheels turn and lands once on 4,980 mi (DD-16); and the single live accent per viewport belongs to the ACTION (the persistent CTA and live telemetry), not to atmosphere (DD-13). Cars are always suggested by silhouette; no brand marks appear on screen or in sketches.

## Director’s decisions

Each is a fork where the dictated script met the locked brand. Recommendation is what the draft currently commits to; anything still yours to rule on is echoed in Open Questions.

### DD-01 — Chosen-car identity across the ignition-through-coast run — resolve the R8/Senna flip-flop the red team flagged as a continuity blocker (you currently get INTO a Senna at SB-08/09 and sit down in an Audi at SB-10).

_Options:_
- Keep R8(dawn)->S8(dusk) as the single continuous car for ALL of Acts II-VI; re-storyboard SB-08/09 to an R8 (conventional door, shot tight for drama); the Senna stays only as a lineup card
- Commit the ENTIRE ignition-through-coast run (SB-08 through SB-15) to the McLaren Senna for its dihedral door, and reword the 'one continuous car' north star
- Keep hedging per frame (rejected — this is what produced the hard break)

**Recommendation:** Option 1: one continuous R8->S8; the Senna is demoted to a lineup card; SB-08's chosen car, SB-09's door, SB-10's cockpit and SB-13/15's hero all name the R8.

**Why:** The locked north star is 'ONE continuous car/journey,' and every real photo supports R8->S8 while the McLaren F1/720S/675LT have no photo, model or data. Committing to the Senna would force an aspirational car with no clean asset into the emotional lead and require rewording the north star. The Senna's energy is honored as a real lineup card, so nothing is lost; the dihedral-door drama is traded for narrative integrity.

### DD-02 — Lineup casting: real asset-backed cars vs the full aspirational script list (McLaren F1/720S/675LT, Revuelto, Cullinan Black Badge, purple Lambo).

_Options:_
- Cast the full script list including the dream cars
- Cast only real asset-backed cars (R8, Senna, SVJ, Countach, LaFerrari, GT3 RS, RS6), silhouette-only
- Mix real cars with clearly-labeled placeholder plates for dream cars behind a feature flag

**Recommendation:** Option 2 as shipped v1: real-only lineup, suggested by silhouette; hold 2-3 dream slots as flagged to-source plates behind a feature flag for later.

**Why:** The honesty guardrail and the no-logos sketch rule both point to silhouette. A real-only lineup reads as film-noir restraint and avoids shipping invented cars the brand cannot deliver, while the flag preserves a path to add dream cars once assets exist. All cutouts must be angle- and key-normalized (see DD-05 and the Tech Spec) since the source photos vary wildly in angle and light.

### DD-03 — The scenic act has zero coastal footage, and the only real road plate (Telluride) is geographically off the Denver->Miami route.

_Options:_
- License or shoot golden-hour Gulf/coastal drone footage before launch
- Use the real Telluride mountain plate for the whole scenic act
- Render a stylized coast in R3F

**Recommendation:** Use the real Telluride plate ONLY for the mountain road beat (SB-13), labeled honestly as a mountain leg out of Denver's region — NOT as coast; stage SB-14 to SB-16 as a to-source golden-hour Gulf/coast poster; source real Florida-coast footage for v1-launch or v2, and add a bridging cue (light/time shift plus an odometer leg jump) so mountain-to-coast reads as miles covered, not a cut.

**Why:** Denver-to-Miami maps mountain-to-coast, so Telluride is real and on-brand for the origin, but Telluride is in SW Colorado and must not be dressed as the Miami approach or as coast. A synthetic R3F ocean risks a garish, off-brand look for no payoff. Critically, on mobile the canvas is OFF, so the coastal poster is the DEFAULT experience for most of the priority audience — the missing Gulf plate is a mobile ship blocker for Acts V-VI, not a v2 nice-to-have (see DD-12 and Open Questions).

### DD-04 — Crescendo target: booking (script) vs sponsor the wrap.

_Options:_
- End on a plain 'book your drive' CTA as scripted
- Land 'Sponsor the wrap.' as the primary hero with 'Get on the list.' as the quiet secondary
- Present dual equal-weight CTAs

**Recommendation:** Land the sponsor wrap as the hero payload (primary 'Sponsor the wrap.') with 'Get on the list.' as the secondary — and, per the conversion findings, co-locate the ask with the SB-19 wrap peak rather than deferring it to a separate terminal frame.

**Why:** Sponsors are the number-one conversion and the wrap pitch (one car, ten markets, 5,000 miles) is the tangible ask. Deferring the only sponsor CTA to SB-20 means peak sponsor intent at SB-19 occurs with no button on screen; intent and action must be co-visible (see DD-14). The secondary must point at the existing /apply route, not the nonexistent /list route (verified: no app/list exists).

### DD-05 — 3D GLB models vs photo-parallax for the fleet and scenes.

_Options:_
- Source or build a sub-2-3MB Draco/meshopt GLB fleet plus HDRI
- Photo-parallax layers + video + 2D depth, reserving WebGL for atmosphere and the gauge only
- Hybrid: one hero GLB for the chosen car, photos for the rest

**Recommendation:** Photo-parallax + video for the whole spine (car cutouts on hairline-separated Z planes); reserve the one sanctioned WebGL risk for atmosphere and the procedural gauge-sweep. A hero GLB can be added later behind the same island.

**Why:** No GLB or HDRI exist anywhere in the repo; a brand-compliant fleet is a multi-week asset effort. Photo-parallax ships now with real cars and satisfies the GPU-cheap fallback gate. Every cutout must be capped (<=1600px longest edge), compressed/KTX2 with mipmaps, and held to a per-viewport texture-memory budget — the multi-cutout frames (SB-04, SB-07, SB-08) otherwise blow mobile VRAM; on mobile, prefer the flat poster over instantiating 5-10 planes.

### DD-06 — Flex vs restraint in the driving and card beats.

_Options:_
- Full script flex (neon accents, big stat reveals, 'feel the power' hype)
- Convert to Centurion restraint — one live accent per viewport, no stat-brag, film-noir grade
- Middle path: restrained grade but keep animated stat counters

**Recommendation:** Full restraint: keep the cinematic arc, remove the flex; power is implied by grade, motion and telemetry, never shouted. The 'feel the power' beat (SB-13) lands as the master tagline over motion, not as a hype line.

**Why:** The locked voice and banned-word list forbid the hype outright, and quiet luxury converts the target sponsor and enthusiast better than a brochure flex. Restraint is also what makes the single live accent legible.

### DD-07 — Spec slots on the car cards with no data model.

_Options:_
- Invent plausible 0-60/hp/top-speed figures
- Keep a designed spec slot filled with honest placeholder labels until a fleet data model exists
- Drop specs entirely from the cards

**Recommendation:** Keep the spec slot but fill it with honest placeholder labels ('spec — to confirm' / 'full specs at launch'); never invent numbers.

**Why:** No specs and no fleet data model exist in the repo, and the canonical-facts guardrail forbids invention. The slot preserves the card layout for real data later, and the honest label reads as restraint rather than as a gap.

### DD-08 — Windscreen-to-road transition mechanic.

_Options:_
- Literal wiper-blade sweep
- Light-wipe / exposure bloom from dark cabin to bright road
- Hard cut

**Recommendation:** Light-wipe: dawn light blooms across the glass and resolves to open road. This is also where the light arc crosses from pre-dawn to day and where the odometer begins counting miles.

**Why:** A literal wiper reads gimmicky against the quiet-luxury tone; a light-wipe matches the sunrise grade and the KINETIC mask-up language, is trivially GPU-cheap, and gives the odometer a motivated 'wheels first turn here' start (DD-16).

### DD-09 — Wrap-reveal fidelity and the SB-18->SB-19 transition, given the missing clean S8 side profile AND the corrected truth that the only S8 photo is a gunmetal, midday, no-livery car (the grounding brief mislabels it 'dusk heritage-livery').

**Recommendation:** Option 1 for v1: regrade the real gunmetal S8 to dusk, composite an authored line-art/SVG champagne livery (the livery is NOT a photographic crop — it does not exist in the pixels), and replace the hard jump-cut with a camera arc/orbit that carries the S8 on-screen from SB-18's crane-back to a true side profile. **Why:** The clean evenly-lit S8 side profile needed for the money frame does not exist, and the heritage stripe is not in any source photo — so both SB-16's stripe macro and SB-19's reveal are authored overlays plus a day-to-dusk regrade, a much larger task than 'clean masking.' On a page whose north star is 'one continuous gesture,' the sponsor money frame cannot be reached by the only hard jump-cut in the spine; carrying the car through a motivated orbit keeps scale/orientation change watched, not cut. SB-16 and SB-19 must tell the same asset truth.

### DD-10 — Scope of the one sanctioned WebGL risk (BUILD-SPEC section 9).

_Options:_
- Full 3D scene (garage, fly-through, and cars all in WebGL)
- WebGL only for atmosphere (fog, breathing Gulf radial, grain) plus the model-free procedural gauge-sweep; photo/video/CSS carry the rest

**Recommendation:** Scope WebGL to the atmospheric backdrop and the procedural gauge-sweep (SB-11), which are procedural with zero assets to source and degrade cleanly to a poster; photo-parallax, video and CSS carry everything else.

**Why:** A full 3D scene needs the GLB fleet we do not have and risks the SSR and perf gates. Atmosphere plus the vector/shader gauge is GPU-cheap and honors the 'one place WebGL risk is worth it behind a GPU-cheap fallback' mandate. Note this is greenfield, not a reuse of an existing island (DD-11).

### DD-11 — Page and scroll architecture — reconcile the brief's 'reuse the existing single R3F island and shared scrollYProgress' with the verified repo, where none of that exists.

_Options:_
- Assume the existing GarageStage island and a shared scroll value and 'slot in' (rejected — verified false)

**Recommendation:** Option 2: a brand-new spine route. Add three + @react-three/fiber (drei/meshopt only if used) as a next/dynamic {ssr:false} island; expose ONE useScroll on the spine root; feed that one 0->1 value to the canvas, the Odometer, and every frame remap. **Why:** Verified: package.json has only framer-motion + lenis (no three/fiber/drei); GarageStage is a pure DOM/CSS sibling layer with an always-on rAF, not the WebGL island; there is no shared scrollYProgress (GarageStage uses whole-document useScroll while RoadbookStage/Room/ArrivalScene are section-scoped); and GarageStage lives on / while RoadbookStage+Odometer live on /tour — they are two routes, not one page. Every 'reuse the existing' instruction describes infrastructure that must be built. This is new construction, and must be estimated as such.

The current storyboard invests detail in the 'live' path while leaving four posters un-sourced (SB-14/15/17/18) and others as 'export a render' TBDs — that inverts the business priority. The posters are the product for most sponsors; WebGL is the reward for capable desktops.

## Global visual spec

THE SINGLE LIVE ACCENT (Centurion restraint, one per viewport) NOW BELONGS TO THE ACTION, not the atmosphere (DD-13): Gulf Blue #6CBDE6 (hover #8ED0F0) is reserved for the persistent CTA and live telemetry (the counting odometer tick, the ignition needle at SB-11, the chosen-car live state). LIGHT ARC. The grade travels with the story: pre-dawn from SB-01; dawn warming to day at the windscreen light-wipe (SB-12, DD-08); cooling to dusk from arrival (SB-17). Note the two real hero plates are OVERCAST autumn light (R8-telluride) and MIDDAY desert daylight (S8-tortillaflats) — every 'golden hour' / 'dawn peak' / 'dusk' claim is a required REGRADE step, not a property of the source pixels.

TYPE DISCIPLINE. Bricolage Grotesque for display and the odometer; Schibsted Grotesk for body and telemetry; exactly ONE Spectral-italic jewel line PER SCENE — and the device is now RARE: jewels are thinned to roughly half the frames so the peaks land (silence works the rest). ALL kickers are sentence-case (fix the five lowercase offenders: SB-03/04/05/07/12). No uppercase kicker labels, no emoji, no multiple exclamation points, no ALL-CAPS for emphasis.

MOTION. KINETIC mask-up reveals, scroll-scrubbed on the signature cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1) with lerp 0.12 + smoothstep. Cars are always suggested by SILHOUETTE; no brand marks anywhere on screen or in sketches.

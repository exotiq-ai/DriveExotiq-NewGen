### Film menu sheet overflows and clips on phones with no way to scroll
- **Lens:** touch-interaction · **File:** components/experience/FilmMenu.tsx:136
- **Detail:** The sheet is `fixed inset-0` with an inner `flex h-full flex-col justify-center px-8 pb-16` and NO overflow-y-auto, while open state sets `document.body.style.overflow='hidden'` (line 53). - **Fix:** Add `overflow-y-auto` to the sheet and swap `justify-center` for `my-auto` on the content (safe centering), or use `justify-center` with `min-h-full` inside a scroll container.
- **Verifier:** Every claim verifies against the code.

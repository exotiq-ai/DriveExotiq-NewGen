# Drive Exotiq — Award-Level Concept Panel

Five interactive prototypes (one per creative direction) generated by a subagent panel, judged and synthesized. Open any `concept-*.html` in a browser and scroll.

## Ranking

## Recommendation

## Build `journey` — Roadbook: The Page Is The Drive

This is the only concept where the **interaction model and the business story are the same object**. Scroll equals miles; the page equals the Denver→Miami tour. That earns the SOTD award on the lever juries reward most — a single committed conceit, legible in three seconds, that rewards the full scroll with synchronized craft (route-draw + odometer + image cross-dissolve + temperature drift + pinned city beats, all locked to one `jp` progress value). Crucially, it is the only direction that **converts all three audiences with one mechanic**:

- **Sponsors (priority 1):** They scroll the actual asset they'd be buying — one car, 5,000 miles, ten named markets arriving as Gulf-blue nodes. The wrap pitch writes itself: \"your livery on this car, down this line, through these ten cities.\" The route rail is a media-value map. No other concept makes the sponsorship *tangible* the way watching the S8 travel the line does.
- **Enthusiasts:** The odometer, the heritage-livery S8 sleeper, the rally-roadbook rail speak the language of people who actually drive.
- **Cold QR walk-ups:** Self-explanatory in one gesture. Unlike `sunrise` (dark/silent first frame can read as broken on a phone with no scroll cue) it's immediately legible — they see a road and a map and understand instantly.

### Honest tradeoffs
- **The cross-dissolve is the entire risk.** The two stills (`./r8.jpg` mountain-dawn, `./s8.jpg` desert-dusk) must be color-graded to feel like one continuous drive, or the two-thirds-mark cut reads as \"two photos.\" In production this should become a driving plate or a WebGL forward-displaced road plane (the one place WebGL risk is worth it — the grair energy). Ship the DOM/SVG/scrub version first; treat WebGL as a phase-2 upgrade, not a launch dependency.
- **Length.** An 11×–and-up viewport scroll needs a persistent progress meter so users know the length is intentional. Build it in from day one.
- **The sky temperature shift is deliberately subtle** and may be missed at a glance — lean on the low radial Gulf glow breath and the image grade, never on saturation (that would break quiet-luxury).

### How to fold in the best of the losers
- **From `machine`:** give each city node a tiny \"build-sheet\" telemetry detail (leg miles, an HP/sound stat on the S8) so enthusiasts get gearhead credibility *inside* the journey, and give sponsors a dedicated `/sponsor` beat with the wrap pitch and the route as media-value map.
- **From `sunrise`:** open the journey at literal dawn — the first 8% intro is the pre-dawn idle, and the first city (Denver) arrives as first light breaks. You get `sunrise`'s emotional cold-open *and* `journey`'s legibility, resolving `journey`'s only audience risk.
- **From `kinetic`:** steal its mask-up reveal discipline for the city names so the typography traveling past the car is as crafted as a print object.
- **From `garage`:** if the WebGL road upgrade happens, borrow the streaming dust-mote + 5% grain film layer for volumetric weight.

Net: `journey` as the spine, a `sunrise` cold-open as the intro beat, `machine`'s telemetry as the per-city payload, `kinetic`'s type craft throughout.

## Build plan

## Build Plan — `journey` (Roadbook) in this Next.js 14 App Router repo

The repo is already provisioned for this almost suspiciously well, which de-risks the build:
- **`lenis@1.3.25` and `framer-motion@12` are already installed** (`package.json`) — no new core deps for the scrub.
- **Locked tokens are live as CSS vars** in `app/globals.css` / `tailwind.config.ts`: `gulf` is action-only, 2px corners enforced (`borderRadius.DEFAULT: \"2px\"`), hairlines `line`/`line-2`, no glow. Use these classes directly — do not re-declare hex.
- **All three fonts are self-hosted woff2** in `app/fonts/` (Bricolage variable, Schibsted variable, Spectral 400/500 + italics) wired via `--font-display/--font-sans/--font-serif`. The italic roadbook voice lines and odometer are free.
- **`app/sitemap.ts` already anticipates `/tour` and `/tour/[city]`** in its own comment. The crawlable city pages are a planned route, not a fight.

### Tech choices
- **Smooth scroll:** existing Lenis, wrapped in a client `<SmoothScrollProvider>` mounted in a client layout segment (keep the root `app/layout.tsx` server). Lenis raf feeds a single shared progress value.
- **Scrub engine:** prefer **GSAP ScrollTrigger** for the pinned scrub timeline (snapping/pin reliability beats hand-rolled rAF). It is the one new dep worth adding. If you want zero new deps, Framer Motion `useScroll`/`useTransform` + a manual lerp (factor 0.12) reproduces the weighted feel — acceptable, slightly more wiring for the pinned beat.
- **Route line:** native inline **SVG `strokeDashoffset`** bound to progress; warm-metal (`metal`) car dot via `getPointAtLength(jp)`; ten nodes flip hairline→`gulf` as passed. Cheap, razor-sharp at any DPR.
- **Odometer:** tabular-nums counter (`tabular-nums`) tweened off the same progress, Bricolage.
- **Images:** two `next/image` layers (`priority`, blur placeholder), cross-dissolved via opacity at the two-thirds mark; **preload both** to avoid a swap flash. `object-fit: cover`, framed top/bottom by bg-gradient vignette so it reads as a horizon strip, not a photo. `will-change: transform`; cap the 1.06→1.22 scale range (full-bleed scale is fill-rate heavy).
- **Motion law:** transform/opacity only, single global lerp 0.12, smoothstep `t*t*(3-2t)` on every reveal, one rAF loop. 60fps target at 1440×900.

### Phase 1 — Signature moment first (the recordable wow), ~3–4 days, low risk
1. Scaffold `app/tour/page.tsx` as a server component holding **all ten city beats as real server-rendered `<section>`s with `<h2>` city names + leg copy** (crawlable, deep-linkable). A client `<RoadbookStage>` child only *animates* what the server already rendered — content is never JS-injected.
2. Build the pinned windshield stage: forward image push + cross-dissolve, SVG roadbook rail with `getPointAtLength` car dot, ten nodes, odometer 000000→4,980, subtle RGB-interpolated sky drift with the low radial Gulf glow breath, 5% SVG grain overlay.
3. City beats: fade-up-from-below / hold-center / continue-upward (the `kinetic` mask-up discipline) so words travel past the car.
4. Persistent progress meter (leg counter in `gulf`) so the long scroll reads as intentional.
5. **`sunrise` cold-open as the intro (first 8%):** pre-dawn idle → Denver arrives on first light. Resolves the cold-walk-up legibility risk while stealing the emotional opener.
6. Finale: Miami beat rises over last 10% → **one Gulf-blue CTA** (`Request the drive` / sponsor inquiry). Exactly one accent moment per screen, Centurion restraint.

### Phase 2 — Depth + audience payloads, ~2–3 days
- `/tour/[city]` dynamic routes; add the ten paths to `app/sitemap.ts` (the comment already plans this).
- Per-city **`machine` telemetry detail** (leg miles, S8 HP/sound) for gearhead credibility.
- Dedicated **`/sponsor` beat**: the route rail rendered as a media-value map (markets × reach), wrap pitch, contact CTA — the sponsor conversion surface.

### Phase 3 (optional, +3–4 days, moderate risk) — the WebGL upgrade
- Replace the two CSS image plates with an **R3F / WebGL forward-displaced road plane + depth haze** for true volumetric push (the grair energy) and to eliminate the two-photo cross-dissolve risk. Borrow `garage`'s streaming motes + grain. Gate behind a GPU-cheap fallback to the proven Phase-1 DOM/SVG path.

### Mobile + SEO/crawlability (must-haves, not afterthoughts)
- **SEO:** all city copy and headings server-rendered in semantic sections; animation is progressive enhancement only. Decorative SVG/grain `aria-hidden`. `/tour/[city]` deep-linkable. This is the concept's structural advantage — the narrative units are also the URL units.
- **Mobile:** cap/clamp the image scale range and shorten the per-beat scroll distance below ~760px; ship a simplified stacked variant (vertical city list + static route) under small viewports and `prefers-reduced-motion`. Honor reduced-motion by snapping lerp to 1 (clean scrub, no easing drift) without gating any content.
- **Recording:** a steady top→bottom capture lands every beat because everything is scrubbed to scroll position, not time — exactly what a SOTD reel needs.

### Rough effort
Launchable signature (Phase 1): **3–4 days** senior creative dev, low risk, ships now. Full audience-complete build (Phase 2): **+2–3 days**. WebGL hero (Phase 3): **+3–4 days**, optional, moderate risk — not required to win.
